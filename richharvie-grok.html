THE ARTIST

Rich Harvie

For over fifteen years, Rich Harvie has been capturing the quiet poetry of the modern world. His work has been exhibited at the Victoria & Albert Museum, the Guggenheim, and featured in National Geographic, The New York Times, and The Guardian.

Harvie’s signature approach — dramatic chiaroscuro and an almost spiritual attention to light — has earned him the Sony World Photography Award (2023) and the Royal Photographic Society’s Gold Medal.
